07 · Companion

Meet Lumi

Reduced-motion safe

Lumi is a celestial companion — a soft, rounded star with a warm spark at its crown. Six states, one calm personality. Only idle loops (a slow float and glow); every other state plays briefly and rests.

Resting light: a slow float and a soft glow.

Idle

Waits quietly beside the child. Gentle breathing only.

Listening

Leans in while a question or word is read aloud.

Thinking

Ponders briefly while the child works, then settles.

Helping

Steps in with a hint or a "Show me" explanation.

Encouraging

Praises effort after any try — not only correct answers.

Celebrating

A brief burst for milestones, then back to idle.

Exploring a new Lumi? Three celestial crescent/star concepts are ready to compare — each shown across all six states, at icon scale, and in dark mode. This Lumi stays exactly as it is until you choose.

What each option does

Reading again supports listening; "Show me" supports visual learners; breaking into sounds supports decoding; a simpler word lowers the challenge without lowering the goal; a break protects attention. The adaptive engine records which help was used — never as a penalty, only to choose better support next time.

8 · Lumi companion interaction  ·  path: present across every screen

How Lumi shows up
I'm here whenever you need me. No rush.

Resting light: a slow float and a soft glow.

Lumi never scores, ranks or compares. Lumi is a friend beside you, not a teacher marking your work.

When each state appears in the product

Idle

Beside every reading screen, waiting calmly.

Listening

While a question is read aloud, or when the child taps "Read it to me".

Thinking

Briefly while the child works, then rests so it never nags.

Helping

Opens the help flow or a "Show me" explanation.

Encouraging

After effort — a try, a correction, or a helpful question.

Celebrating

Finishing a set or a week of reading. Never a leaderboard.
