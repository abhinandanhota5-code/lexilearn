How they differ

Crescent Bloom
Warmest and calmest; the moon itself is the character.
Starling
Most distinctive and most "guiding light"; boldest silhouette.
Moonglow
Most expressive — the star adds a second moving element for states.

All three keep Lumi Teal as the body with a warm amber celestial accent, a simple expressive face, and the same six states. Only idle floats/glows in a loop; every other state plays briefly and rests. Motion collapses under reduced-motion.
